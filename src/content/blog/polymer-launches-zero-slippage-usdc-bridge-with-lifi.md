Polymer Labs and LI.FI are joining forces to co-launch a groundbreaking zero slippage USDC bridge for the first time ever on Jumper Exchange. Any user can swap USDC 1:1 across 16 chains (more on the way), including Ethereum, Base, HyperEVM, Arbitrum, Solana, and more.

**Experience zero slippage bridging here:** [*https://jumper.exchange/*](https://jumper.exchange/)

![](/assets/blog/polymer-launches-zero-slippage-usdc-bridge-with-lifi/LIFI_CCTP_Blog_Graphic_1.png)

Moving USDC across chains with traditional bridging options often lead to significant value erosion to third parties, or the need to manage complex relayer infrastructure. Polymer’s implementation flips the script by offering a truly lossless way to move liquidity across chains. 

![](/assets/blog/polymer-launches-zero-slippage-usdc-bridge-with-lifi/LIFI_CCTP_Blog_Graphic_2.png)

To date, the bridge has already processed multiple seven figure swaps without any value erosion—now available to all via Jumper’s UI.

> Recognizing a path to zero slippage, we partnered with [LI.FI](http://li.fi) to merge their extensive market penetration with Polymer's Execute API to offer a truly positive-sum, efficient alternative in a space often focused on value extraction. We are incredibly excited to share this with the public.

The partnership also spawned a second bridge route with 20-second finality at a low, fixed 2bps fee, optimized for both speed and cost as a “Fast” option.

### **Powered by Polymer: Execute API**

Polymer’s Execute API acts as the dedicated relayer, handling the on-chain submission of transactions to ensure swift, reliable delivery. Leveraging this infrastructure, users simply select their preferred Polymer route on the Jumper UI, and the Execute API ensures the transaction is finalized on the destination chain.

**Key Benefits**

- **Zero-Loss Transfers:** Users can move large amounts of USDC without worrying about slippage or hidden spreads across 16 chains (more on the way).

- **Predictable Execution:** The "Fast" option provides a reliable 20-second path for those who need speed without the premium costs of other fast-bridges.

- **Unified Destination:** This integration cements LI.FI and Jumper as the premier destination for USDC bridging, offering the most competitive rates and latencies powered by Polymer.
