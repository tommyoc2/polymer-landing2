Polymer is proud to celebrate Superform Labs’ US$1.4M Echo community round. Led by the ‘Polymer Pals’ Echo group, the raise will advance Superform’s product development roadmap and mainstream adoption of on-chain financial tools—starting with Superform’s Desktop V2.

Desktop v2 makes the on-chain earning experience as simple, fluid, and accessible as traditional finance apps. Users can access crosschain yield strategies from 60+ protocols via one interface, without any of the hassle of managing a crosschain portfolio. 

Polymer will make unified crosschain liquidity accessible at subsecond speeds and a fraction of the cost for Superform users. This collaboration is expected to drive significant crosschain volume for Polymer, while empowering Superform to scale future products without compromising performance. 

The raise reaffirms Polymer’s commitment to win-win outcomes with our collaborators. We look forward to driving the next wave of crypto adoption by enabling scalable, high-performance consumer-facing applications like Superform.
